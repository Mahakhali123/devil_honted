5. LAST CHANGE - BEDROOM SYSTEMS (IMPLEMENTED, NOT APPROVED)
====================================================================
All of this is in tests/step1_duplex_palace.gd unless noted.

A. Netted Palang
- Replaced the old bed/canopy calls with a custom _palang() builder.
- Heavy four-poster, raised mattress, real crawlspace under the bed,
  translucent dusty mosquito-net walls/ceiling and torn net flap.
- Hide point on the north side of the bed.
- PlayerController.enter_hiding_under_bed()/exit_hiding_under_bed()
  teleport under the bed, disable collision, lower the camera, and set
  GameManager.player_hidden.
- Player can look up while hidden. A full-screen shader "net veil"
  distorts the view, draws netting, and blends in a vague ghost head/
  shoulders when Kamla is nearby.
- Kamla ignores/leaves a hidden player: enemy_ghost.gd checks
  GameManager.player_hidden in sensing/chase/attack/strike.

B. Antique Almirah
- Custom _antique_almirah() with openable hinged doors and a dark
  interior. Rusty hinges.
- Opening emits a 32 m noise spike through
  GameEventBroker.player_noise_made.
- Loot order: matches, rusted key, camphor sachet, then dust.
- 32% chance to reveal a huddled weeping-girl figure inside, with
  sanity/threat cost.

C. Tarnished Vanity Mirror
- Broken glass bangles, dried sindoor, tarnished wall mirror.
- Gazing reveals the randomized three-digit sandook code and pale
  "safe path" floor marks.
- Staring drains sanity after 2 seconds; at 3.4 seconds the player's
  low-poly reflection appears with a malicious smile.
- Forced break after about 8 seconds costs extra sanity.

D. Iron-Bound Sandook
- Large iron-strapped chest at the foot of the palang, brass padlock,
  three dials, lid that opens.
- Interaction freezes the player with PlayerController.set_frozen(true)
  while the world/ghost keep running.
- Code UI: A/D turn, W/S choose dial, Enter try, Q step back.
- Wrong attempts make noise and cost threat/sanity.
- Fumbling makes periodic noise, so the player is vulnerable from behind.
- Correct code gives 2 matchboxes and an iron key stamped KAMLA.
- The code is randomized at scene build and is revealed by the mirror.

E. Portrait Wall
- Four rich ghost-photo frames on the ground bedroom west wall.
- As sanity falls, the people disappear from the glass:
  royal couple <55, civilian couple <38, King <28, Queen <20.
- Photos return if sanity recovers.
- This required the shared _rich_photo_frame() in step1_duplex.gd to
  name its photo mesh "Photograph".

F. Brass Surahi
- Brass pitcher on a new nightstand beside the palang.
- Drinking restores 48 stamina.
- If the player was recently in the Black Magic room, or carries cursed
  items, the water is corrupted: stamina is set to zero, sanity/threat
  rise, and a screen-space blur/tint runs for about 4.8 seconds.
- 16-second refill cooldown.

G. New bedroom preview angles added
  bedroom, bedroomantique, bedroommirror, bedroomportraits,
  bedroomalmirah
Only this still has been rendered so far:
  preview/step1_palace_bedroom.png
It shows the new palang and sandook, but the room still reads very dark;
lighting/composition needs review.
